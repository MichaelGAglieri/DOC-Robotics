The downloaded LICENSE.txt files do not specify a Creative Commons version, so this release does not invent one. Thingiverse pages could not be independently retrieved during preparation; the table reports the notices present in the local packages. Obtain the files and current license terms from the author. A public download does not give permission to relicense it under GPL or MIT.

The repository deliberately contains links rather than copies of these CAD/STL/3MF assets. Any later publication of modified parts requires a separate provenance and permissions review.
